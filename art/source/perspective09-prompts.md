## Cottage generation

Generate a single complete cozy woodland cottage, front elevation seen from a camera 25 degrees above horizontal, slight perspective, only about 8 degrees to the right of the front so a narrow RIGHT side wall is visible. The roof top is visible at a shallow angle, not an isometric diamond and not a 45-degree overhead view. Front door faces the viewer. Whole building and all eaves and chimney fully inside the frame with transparent padding. Design: intimate one-and-a-half-storey fairytale cottage, weathered warm red-brown tiled gable roof, a small stone chimney, plaster and dark timber upper facade, blue-gray stone lower walls, rounded wooden door, three small cozy amber windows, modest wooden flower boxes attached to facade. Thick crafted architecture with irregular warm worn edges. Rich fine pixel-art clusters and material detail, Japanese HD-2D diorama aesthetics, painterly pixel craft, nostalgic substantial storybook warmth. Crisp discrete pixels and controlled dark outlines, no airbrushed surfaces, no smooth vector or low-poly render look, no plastic highlights. Muted moss/ochre/blue-gray palette, warm soft illumination from upper left. Building albedo/detail lighting only; NO long ground shadow, no glow halo, no smoke. No people, trees, separate grass, garden, fence, road, background scene, circular island, broad ground platform, words, labels, checkerboard or watermark. Visible stone foundation at the lowest building edge, a small attached doorstep only. Ground contact is a clear base line. Real transparent alpha outside the cottage. This will be displayed about 220-350 screen pixels wide, so provide readable clustered details rather than blurry noise.

## Grass generation

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent pixel-art grass sprite sheet for a 2.5D HD-2D woodland adventure. Exactly 4 columns by 2 rows of equally sized cells. Exactly one separate natural grass tuft centered in each cell, nothing else. No grid lines or labels. Row 1: four short low meadow-grass variations, compact narrow pointed overlapping blades with several visible tips. Row 2: four slightly taller meadow-grass variations, gently arching narrow leaves, NOT ferns, NOT bushes, NOT round puffs. Each tuft grows upward from one compact root point at bottom center of its own cell, consistent root baseline at about 86 percent down its cell; generous transparent separation around each tuft. Fixed view: camera 25 degrees above horizontal, grass is upright, roots visible just enough to anchor, restrained depth within each cluster. Medium-dark muted olive and sage, dusty moss green, small warm straw highlights at blade tips and cool dark root shadows. Rich readable crisp pixel clusters matching a detailed hand-pixelled woodland cottage, restrained outlines. Visible small groups of leaves, not disconnected one-pixel confetti. No neon/chartreuse lawn colors, no flowers, no dirt bases, no rocks, no insects, no blades lying flat, no round grass balls, no 3D render, no blur, no airbrush, no checkerboard background, no text. Truly transparent alpha between and around all eight sprites. Need game-ready consistent separate silhouettes.
