## Complete generation prompt

Use case: game-texture. Generate one square seamless tileable albedo texture for natural bedrock cliff faces in a cozy detailed painterly fantasy RPG. This is a production material, NOT a scene illustration or a rock prop. Orthographic straight-on flat pigment map, equal ambient illumination everywhere, no top face, no perspective, no directional baked lighting or cast shadows, no ambient occlusion gradients. Medium scale irregular angular vertical/diagonal fractured rock plates in restrained warm gray and blue-gray slate, approximately 8 to 12 irregular large planes across the tile, fine sparse hairline joints, subtle mineral grain in hand painted gouache / soft pixel brush style. Read as continuous solid exposed geology, not masonry, not stacked bricks, not cobbles, not rounded stones. Medium value overall, narrow restrained range; keep individual rock faces mostly calm readable solid color rather than busy mottled camouflage. Moss essentially absent except tiny very desaturated pinches in a few deepest cracks, under 2 percent coverage. No grass, foliage, soil, glowing seams, text, borders, grid, tileset sheet, green wash. All four edges must wrap seamlessly. Fill entire square edge to edge with rock pigment, opaque background. Target a texture that can be mapped onto actual angular 3D rock geometry with independent lighting, so do not paint dramatic highlights, extruded boulder silhouettes or top-versus-side lighting.
